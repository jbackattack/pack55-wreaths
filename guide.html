Getting started

Open the app on your phone (save it to your home screen so it's one tap away):
https://pack55-wreaths.vercel.app/

  1. Type your Scout's name

    Start typing and pick the name from the list. Use the Scout's own name, not the parent's.

  2. Enter your 4-digit PIN

    The first time you sign in, you'll be asked to choose a new 4-digit PIN. Pick something you'll remember and write it down.

  3. Choose how you're selling

    Door-to-door is always available. The table sale buttons only work during their sale weekends.
Uniform on. Scouts should wear their uniform whenever they're selling. Always have a parent with you.

Door-to-door sales

  1. Tap the items

    Tap a product once for each one the customer wants. Use the minus button if you tap too many. The total updates as you go.

  2. Fill in customer info

    Name, address and phone number are required so we can deliver. Email and notes are optional. The address box grows as you type, so long addresses are fine.

  3. Choose how they're paying

    Cash
    Take the cash now. Never promise to collect later.
    Zelle
    The Pack's Zelle QR code shows on screen. Tap it to enlarge so the customer's phone can scan it. Confirm the payment arrived before you finish.
    Check
    Payable to Cub Scout Pack 55.

  4. Complete the sale

    You'll see a confirmation. Offer the customer a receipt: tap Text or Email and the message opens on your phone, ready to send. Then tap Next Sale.
Check your work. Open My Sales any time to see every sale you've entered as its own entry, with your running totals. Do this after each outing so mistakes are caught early.
Collect payment right away. Don't chase it later, and don't enter a sale that hasn't been paid for.

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my PIN.

Ask a pack leader. They can reset it, and you'll choose a new 4-digit PIN the next time you sign in.

I can't find my Scout's name.

Try the first few letters of the first name. If it's still missing, tell a leader so they can add your Scout to the roster.

I entered a sale by mistake, or entered it twice.

Don't try to fix it yourself. Tell a pack leader which sale it was (check My Sales for the time and customer). Leaders can remove individual sales so your totals stay correct.

A wreath shows as sold out.

Inventory is shared across all Scouts and updates live. If you're sure it's wrong, tell a leader. Don't promise delivery of an item the app says is gone.

The Saint James or Somerset Hills button is locked or won't open.

That's on purpose. Leaders turn each table sale on only during its sale hours, and may require a PIN. Ask a leader at your table.

How does a customer pay with Zelle?

Choose Zelle at payment and show the QR code. Tap it to make it larger, and the customer scans it with their banking app. Make sure the payment went through before you tap complete.

Who do checks go to?

Make them payable to Cub Scout Pack 55. Hand checks and cash to a pack leader at the next meeting or pickup.

Does the customer need the app or an account?

No. Only Scouts and parents use the app. Customers just pay and, if they like, get a receipt by text or email from your phone.

How do receipts work?

After completing a sale, tap Text or Email. Your phone's messaging or mail app opens with the receipt filled in, and you press send. Nothing goes out until you do.

What if I don't have internet at the door?

The app needs a connection to record sales and check inventory. If you lose signal, wait until you have it back before completing the sale. Write the order on paper if you need to, then enter it afterward.

Can parents see other Scouts' sales?

No. You only see your own Scout's sales. Pack-wide totals and controls are for leaders.

What is the Donated Wreath for Veterans?

Customers buy a wreath for $25 that we donate through Community Hope NJ to disabled and homeless veterans at the Lyons VA Medical Center in Basking Ridge. Last year the pack donated 60 wreaths for their therapy program's decoration party.

Can I sell outside our neighborhood or online?

Stick to friends, family and neighbors, with a parent along. Ask a leader before selling anywhere else.
